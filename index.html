Who We Are

Eyandradhai Seivom is a Chennai-based charitable trust committed to creating lasting change in the lives of the most vulnerable members of our community. Founded with deep compassion and a spirit of service, we work tirelessly to uplift children, empower women, and care for our elders.

We believe that every child deserves education, every woman deserves opportunity, and every elder deserves dignity. Our dedicated team of volunteers and trustees work across Chennai and surrounding areas of Tamil Nadu to make this vision a reality — one life at a time.

Whether it is providing free tuition to school children, training women with livelihood skills, or ensuring that elderly individuals receive nutritious meals and medical care, Eyandradhai Seivom stands as a pillar of hope for those who need it most.

What We Do

Three pillars of service that define our mission

Education

We provide free tuition, school supplies, uniforms, and scholarships to deserving children — ensuring no child is deprived of the right to learn.

Learn More

Women Empowerment

Through skill training, self-help groups, and financial literacy programs, we enable women to achieve economic independence and lead dignified lives.

Learn More

Helping Elders

We provide food, medicines, free health check-ups, and companionship to destitute senior citizens — giving them the love and care they deserve.
