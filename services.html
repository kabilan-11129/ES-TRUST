Education

Educating the Future of Tomorrow

Education is the most powerful tool for breaking the cycle of poverty. At Eyandradhai Seivom, we believe that every child — regardless of their family's financial situation — deserves access to quality education and a bright future.

We work directly with children from low-income families across Chennai, providing them with the resources, support, and encouragement they need to stay in school and thrive academically.

  • Free after-school tuition and academic support for children from Classes 1 to 10
  • Annual distribution of school bags, notebooks, pens and stationery
  • Provision of school uniforms for deserving students
  • Merit-based scholarships to encourage academic excellence
  • Awareness campaigns to prevent school dropouts among girls
  • Career guidance and digital literacy programs for older students

Women Empowerment

Empowering Women, Strengthening Families

When a woman is empowered, her entire family and community benefits. Eyandradhai Seivom is deeply committed to enabling women from disadvantaged backgrounds to achieve economic independence and social confidence.

Through our skill development workshops, self-help groups, and awareness programs, we equip women with the tools they need to create sustainable livelihoods and lead lives of dignity.

  • Tailoring and garment making training for women seeking employment
  • Formation and support of women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs)
  • Financial literacy and savings management workshops
  • Livelihood support and market linkage for micro-entrepreneurs
  • Awareness programs on women's rights, health, and legal protections
  • Support for women affected by domestic challenges and abandonment

Helping Elders

Caring for Those Who Built Our World

Our elders deserve respect, dignity, and care. Yet many elderly individuals in Chennai — especially those who are destitute or abandoned — live in conditions of extreme hardship, lacking food, medicine, and human connection.

Eyandradhai Seivom stands beside our senior citizens, ensuring they know they are not forgotten. Through regular visits, food drives, medical camps, and companionship, we bring warmth and hope to their lives.

  • Monthly distribution of food rations and groceries to destitute elders
  • Free health check-up camps and provision of essential medicines
  • Regular companionship visits to lonely and abandoned senior citizens
  • Support for elders living on the streets or in unsafe conditions
  • Coordination with hospitals and welfare services for emergency care
  • Festival celebrations and cultural events to bring joy to senior citizens
