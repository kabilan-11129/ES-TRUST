Our Story

Eyandradhai Seivom has been serving the community since 2017. What began as a small group of dedicated individuals with a shared dream — to make a meaningful difference in the lives of the underprivileged — has grown into a recognized charitable trust with programs spanning education, women's livelihood, and elder care.

The trust was founded out of a deep-rooted belief that change begins at the community level. Our founders witnessed firsthand the struggles of children who could not afford school supplies, women who lacked skills to earn independently, and elderly individuals abandoned without care. Moved by these realities, they chose to act.

Today, Eyandradhai Seivom operates across Chennai and parts of Tamil Nadu, running ongoing programs, conducting awareness drives, and partnering with like-minded individuals and organisations to amplify our impact. We are driven not by resources, but by the conviction that every human life has infinite value.

Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

A compassionate society where every child learns freely, every woman flourishes with dignity, and every elder lives with respect and care — regardless of their background or circumstances.

Our Mission

To provide sustainable, community-driven support through education, women's empowerment, and elder care programs that create lasting, positive change in the lives of the underprivileged in Chennai and Tamil Nadu.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Inclusivity and Community — these are the values that guide every action we take. We serve without judgement, work transparently, and celebrate the strength found in collective effort.
